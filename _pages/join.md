The DARoS Lab is **actively recruiting** as it launches at KAIST in February 2026.
We work on dynamic legged and humanoid robots — control, learning, hardware, and
perception — and value people who like to build real systems and run real
experiments.

## Prospective graduate students (MS / PhD)

Admission to the lab is through the
[Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI](https://gsai.kaist.ac.kr/) admissions process.
If you are applying, please email Prof. Kim with:

- **Subject line:** `[Prospective Student] Your Name — Semester`
- A short paragraph on what you want to work on and why DARoS
- Your CV
- Transcript (unofficial is fine)
- Links to any projects, code, or publications

## Interns and undergraduate researchers

We take a small number of interns each term, including international interns. Email
Prof. Kim with your CV, your availability (start/end dates), and what you would like
to work on.

## Postdocs

Reach out directly with your CV, a research statement, and two or three
representative papers.

## Current KAIST students

Send an email or stop by the lab. Prior coursework or projects in robotics,
controls, optimization, or reinforcement learning is helpful but not required.

---

*Note: emails that do not mention anything specific about the lab's research are
usually not answered. A few sentences showing you've looked at our
[publications](/publications/) and [projects](/projects/) go a long way.*
