The DARoS Lab is **actively recruiting** as it launches at KAIST in February 2027.
Our research spans robot hardware design and development, control, perception,
navigation, machine learning, and human–robot interaction (see the
[Research](/projects/) page for details). We value people who like to build real
systems and run real experiments.

## How to apply

**Please submit the form below instead of emailing.** It goes straight to Prof. Kim
and keeps everything in one place.

The form takes a few minutes. It's the right path for **prospective graduate
students (MS / PhD), interns, undergraduate researchers, and postdocs**.

### Have ready before you start

- A short paragraph on what you want to work on and why DARoS
- Your CV
- Transcript (unofficial is fine; not needed for postdocs)
- Links to any projects, code, or publications
- For interns: your available start and end dates
- For postdocs: a brief research statement and two or three representative papers

## Graduate admissions

Joining as an MS/PhD student also requires admission through the
[Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI](https://gsai.kaist.ac.kr/) admissions process.
Submitting the form is not a substitute for applying to the school, but it lets us
know you're interested and start a conversation.

## Current KAIST students

Already at KAIST? Submit the form or stop by the lab. Prior coursework or projects
in robotics, controls, optimization, or reinforcement learning is helpful but not
required.
