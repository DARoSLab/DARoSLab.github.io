Human feet use an articulated toe to extend push-off and manage balance. We build
robots that do the same: **StaccaToe**, a single-leg hopping robot with an actuated
toe that mimics the human leg, and **PresToe**, a humanoid with actuated toes. These
platforms let us study how toe actuation changes the control problem for dynamic
balancing and push-off.
