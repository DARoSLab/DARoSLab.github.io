We are developing a quadruped robot that can perform guide-dog–level guiding work:
leading a handler along a route, negotiating sidewalks and crossings, and avoiding
obstacles using onboard vision alone. The project combines human-centered design
(studying handler–guide-dog interaction), robust vision-only navigation, and quiet,
stable locomotion control suitable for walking next to a person.
