Dynamic robots need actuators that combine high torque density, compact packaging,
and efficient transmission of force. We develop the SLAD (Single LAyer Dual-stage)
cycloidal drive as a high-performance actuator for legged robots. The cycloidal
mechanism is designed to deliver large reductions and high output torque in a
compact package suitable for demanding robotic joints.

We characterize the actuator hardware and its cycloidal disks as part of a broader
effort to build powerful, responsive actuation for dynamic locomotion. The design
supports the balance of mechanical strength, low mass, and controllable output
needed for agile legged systems.
