Fast motion breaks conventional perception. We develop visual odometry and state
estimation that hold up during dynamic legged locomotion — including event-camera
methods with adaptive time-surface representations — and we have released **CEAR**, a
comprehensive event-camera dataset for agile quadruped robots.
