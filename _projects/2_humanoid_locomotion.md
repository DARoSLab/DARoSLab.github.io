We study how humanoids can walk, balance, and recover over discrete and uneven
terrain. Our approach blends model-based whole-body control and trajectory
optimization with reinforcement learning, and extends to biomechanically inspired
skills such as running jumps and soccer kicking. A recurring theme is generic
locomotion policies that transfer across gaits and terrains rather than being tuned
for a single scenario.
