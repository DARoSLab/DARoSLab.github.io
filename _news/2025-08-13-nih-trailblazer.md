Prof. Donghyun Kim has received an **NIH Trailblazer Award** — \$624,064 over three
years from the National Institutes of Health — to develop **robotic guide dogs for
blind and low-vision people**.

Trained guide dogs are expensive to raise and have a short working life, and demand
far outstrips supply. Building on the lab's earlier user-centered studies with guide
dog handlers and trainers, this project targets the capabilities that matter most in
practice: reliable route recall, obstacle detection, stair climbing, longer battery
life, and quiet operation — since users depend heavily on their hearing for safety.
