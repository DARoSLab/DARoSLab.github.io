Prof. Donghyun Kim has received an **NSF CAREER Award** for *"Leveraging Toes for
Energy-Efficient and Agile Humanoid Robot Locomotion."*

Human toes do a lot of quiet work — push-off, balance, adapting to terrain — and
most humanoid robots ignore them entirely. Building on the lab's PresToe and
[StaccaToe](/projects/) platforms, this project develops control algorithms that
give robots real articulated toes and teach them to use them well, aiming to
improve walking efficiency, increase running speed, and unlock more human-like
agility over rough, unstructured terrain — capabilities that matter for disaster
response, infrastructure inspection, and other physically demanding field work.

The five-year, \$750K award was made while the group was at UMass Amherst.
