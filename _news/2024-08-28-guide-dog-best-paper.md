Two pieces of good news from the guide-dog robot project, both led by
**Hochul Hwang**:

- *"Is It Safe to Cross? Interpretable Risk Assessment with GPT-4V for
  Safety-Aware Street Crossing"* was named a **Best Paper Finalist at UR 2024**
  (co-authors Sunjae Kwon and Yekyung Kim).
- A few months earlier, *"Towards Robotic Companions: Understanding
  Handler–Guide Dog Interactions for Informed Guide Dog Robot Design"* received a
  **Best Paper Award at CHI 2024**.

Congratulations to Hochul for leading both efforts. See the
[publications](/publications/) page for the papers.
