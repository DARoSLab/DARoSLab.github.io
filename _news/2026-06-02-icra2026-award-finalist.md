**GuideTWSI** — *"A Diverse Tactile Walking Surface Indicator Dataset from
Synthetic and Real-World Images for Blind and Low-Vision Navigation"* — has been
selected as an **Award Finalist at ICRA 2026**.

The lab has a second paper at ICRA 2026 as well: *"Human-Centered Development of
Guide Dog Robots: Quiet and Stable Locomotion Control"* (oral presentation), which
makes a quadruped guide robot markedly quieter and steadier — important when users
rely on their hearing for safety.
[Noise-reduction video.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-pz_8Hqe6s)

GuideTWSI is led by **Hochul Hwang**; the locomotion paper by **Shangqun Yu**. Both
are part of the guide-dog robot project, with collaborators Soowan Yang, Anh Nguyen,
Parth Goel, Krisha Adhikari, and Trung Dang, and co-advisors Joydeep Biswas,
Sunghoon Ivan Lee, and Nicholas Giudice.

- GuideTWSI: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07060>
- Quiet & Stable Locomotion Control: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11808>
