Congratulations to **Daniel Marew**, the DARoS Lab's first Ph.D. graduate! Daniel
was the first student in the group to design and build custom legged robots and
push them to their dynamic limits — working through no shortage of hardware
failures and mechanical setbacks along the way.

His dissertation, *"From Trajectory Optimization to Learning: Developing Controllers
for Dynamic Legged Robots,"* spans model predictive control, reinforcement learning,
and imitation learning, all demonstrated on real hardware.

Thank you for everything you built here, Daniel — congratulations, and best of luck
in what comes next.
