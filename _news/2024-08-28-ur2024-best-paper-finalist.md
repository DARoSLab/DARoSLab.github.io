*"Is It Safe to Cross? Interpretable Risk Assessment with GPT-4V for Safety-Aware
Street Crossing"* was named a **Best Paper Finalist at UR 2024**. Part of the
guide-dog robot project, the work uses a vision-language model to judge whether a
street crossing is safe and to explain its reasoning.

Led by **Hochul Hwang**, with co-authors Sunjae Kwon and Yekyung Kim. See the
[publications](/publications/) page for the paper.
